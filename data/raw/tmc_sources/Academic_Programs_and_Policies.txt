SECTION 1: INSTITUTIONAL OVERVIEW

1.1 History
Trinidad Municipal College (TMC) was established in 1980 through the vision of Mr. Paciano Petarco, with the support of Municipal Mayor Atty. Avelino N. Puracan. Originally named Trinidad Junior College, it was registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in 1985 with SEC No. 136393. On August 1, 2003, the ownership and management were turned over to the Local Government of Trinidad under the leadership of Mayor Engr. Filadelfo A. Garcia. The institution was renamed Trinidad Municipal College (TMC).

1.2 Legal Basis
- Republic Act 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act, UAQTEA)
- CHED Memorandum Orders (CMOs) on programs and standards
- Local Government Code of the Philippines
- TMC Charter and By-Laws

1.3 Recognition
TMC was recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) as a Higher Education Institution (HEI) pursuant to Commission en banc Resolution No. 564-2020 dated August 18, 2020.

1.4 Governance
TMC is an LGU-managed institution under the Municipality of Trinidad, Bohol. The College is headed by a College President who reports to the Municipal Mayor and the Sangguniang Bayan.

SECTION 2: VISION, MISSION, GOAL, PHILOSOPHY, AND SLOGAN

2.1 Vision
A model institution with fully developed academic, technical-vocational education and skill of manpower with positive work attitudes anchored in the core values of leadership and professionalism essential in the creation of self-reliant citizens.

2.2 Mission
To build well-trained, competent and employable professionals, who will meet the demands of the local and international work places.

2.3 Goal
TMC aims at evolving a whole individual as a child of God and a member of a democratic society who is professionally competent that can provide leadership and advance knowledge, well-trained in a certain vocation not only to help himself but to help others, and practical yet responsible and obedient to the laws of God and to the laws of the government.

2.4 Philosophy
TMC adheres to the philosophy that education is life and growth guided by faith in God and love of fellowmen in an environment of competitiveness, professionalism and excellence.

2.5 Slogan
TMC is committed to public educational services second to none.

SECTION 5: ADMISSION POLICIES

5.1 Admission Requirements for Freshmen
- High school diploma or equivalent
- Transcript of Records (Form 137)
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Birth Certificate (PSA or NSO)
- 2x2 ID pictures (4 copies)
- Medical Certificate
- Barangay Clearance
- Parent/Guardian consent (for minors)

5.2 Admission Requirements for Transferees
- Transfer Credential (Honorable Dismissal)
- Transcript of Records from previous school
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Birth Certificate (PSA or NSO)
- 2x2 ID pictures (4 copies)
- Medical Certificate
- Course description from previous school (for credit evaluation)

5.3 Admission Requirements for Returnees
- Original TOR from TMC
- Clearance from the previous semester
- Medical Certificate (if more than one semester gap)

5.4 Admission Requirements for Cross-Enrollees
- Permit to cross-enroll from home institution
- Transcript of Records from home institution
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Medical Certificate

5.5 Admission Process
Step 1: Submit application and required documents to the Registrar's Office
Step 2: Take the entrance examination (if required)
Step 3: Interview with the Program Head
Step 4: Evaluation of credentials
Step 5: Acceptance notification
Step 6: Enrollment

SECTION 6: RETENTION AND ACADEMIC STANDING

6.1 Grading System
TMC uses the following grading system:
- 1.00 - 1.25: Excellent
- 1.26 - 1.50: Very Good
- 1.51 - 1.75: Good
- 1.76 - 2.00: Satisfactory
- 2.01 - 2.25: Fairly Satisfactory
- 2.26 - 2.50: Passing
- 2.51 - 3.00: Conditional
- 5.00: Failed
- INC: Incomplete
- DRP: Dropped

6.2 Retention Requirements
- Must maintain a General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 2.00
- Must not have more than two (2) grades of 5.00 (Failed) in a semester
- Must not have been subjected to disciplinary action
- Must meet the specific retention requirements of the program

6.3 Academic Probation
- Students with a GWA below 2.00 are placed on academic probation
- Probationary students must improve their GWA within two (2) semesters
- Failure to improve results in academic dismissal

6.4 Academic Dismissal
- Students are academically dismissed if they:
  - Fail to improve their GWA after two (2) semesters of probation
  - Accumulate three (3) semesters of academic probation
  - Fail to meet the program's specific retention requirements

6.5 Re-admission After Dismissal
- Dismissed students may apply for re-admission after one (1) semester
- Re-admission is subject to approval by the Academic Affairs Office
- Re-admitted students are placed on probation for two (2) semesters

SECTION 7: GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS

7.1 General Requirements
- Completion of all required courses in the curriculum
- Minimum GWA as required by the program
- No outstanding financial obligations
- Clearance from all offices
- Submission of all required documents
- Payment of graduation fees

7.2 Program-Specific Requirements
- BEEd and BSEd: Completion of practice teaching and portfolio
- BA PolSci: Completion of internship and research paper
- BA Comm: Completion of internship and portfolio
- BSIT: Completion of capstone project and OJT
- BSCrim: Completion of internship and research paper
- BSOA: Completion of OJT and business plan

7.3 Academic Honors
- Summa Cum Laude: GWA of 1.00 to 1.25
- Magna Cum Laude: GWA of 1.26 to 1.50
- Cum Laude: GWA of 1.51 to 1.75
- With Honors: GWA of 1.76 to 2.00

SECTION 8: ACADEMIC CALENDAR

8.1 Semester System
TMC follows the semester system:
- First Semester: June to October
- Second Semester: November to March
- Summer: April to May

8.2 Academic Calendar Highlights
- Enrollment period: two (2) weeks before the start of classes
- First day of classes: as announced by the Academic Council
- Midterm examination: as per the academic calendar
- Final examination: as per the academic calendar
- Grade submission: within five (5) working days after finals
- Graduation: as announced by the Academic Council
