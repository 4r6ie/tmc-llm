Effective Date: Academic Year 2022-2023
Approved by: Office of the College President

SECTION 1: OFFICE OVERVIEW

1.1 Mission
The Registrar's Office is committed to providing efficient and accurate academic records management and student services to support the academic mission of Trinidad Municipal College.

1.2 Vision
To be a model registrar's office that upholds the highest standards of academic records management, integrity, and student-centered service.

1.3 Functions
The Registrar's Office is responsible for:
- Student enrollment and registration
- Academic records management
- Issuance of official academic documents
- Graduation and credentialing
- CHED compliance and reporting
- Scholarship and financial assistance coordination
- Student information system management

1.4 Staff Composition
- Registrar: Head of the office
- Assistant Registrar: Deputy head of the office
- Enrollment Officers: Handle registration and enrollment processes
- Records Officers: Maintain student academic records
- Document Processing Staff: Handle requests for academic documents
- ICT Support: Manage the student information system

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2: ENROLLMENT AND REGISTRATION

2.1 Enrollment Period
- Regular enrollment: as announced by the Academic Calendar
- Late enrollment: within the first week of classes (with late enrollment fee)
- Drop/add period: within the first two (2) weeks of classes

2.2 Enrollment Process
Step 1: Fill out the enrollment form (available at the Registrar's Office or online portal)
Step 2: Submit required documents:
- Completed enrollment form
- Previous school credentials (TOR, diploma, Form 137)
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Birth certificate (PSA or certified true copy)
- 2x2 ID pictures (4 copies)
- Medical certificate
Step 3: Pay tuition and fees at the Accounting Office
Step 4: Present the official receipt to the Registrar's Office
Step 5: Receive your class schedule and student ID number

2.3 Required Documents for New Students
- High school diploma or equivalent
- Transcript of Records (Form 137)
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Birth Certificate (PSA or NSO)
- 2x2 ID pictures (4 copies)
- Medical Certificate from a licensed physician
- Barangay Clearance
- Parent/Guardian consent form (for minors)

2.4 Required Documents for Transferees
- Transfer Credential (Honorable Dismissal)
- Transcript of Records from previous school
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Birth Certificate (PSA or NSO)
- 2x2 ID pictures (4 copies)
- Medical Certificate
- Course description from previous school (for credit evaluation)

2.5 Cross-Enrollment
- Students from other institutions may cross-enroll at TMC for a maximum of nine (9) units
- A permit to cross-enroll must be obtained from both the home institution and TMC
- Cross-enrolled students must comply with TMC's academic policies

SECTION 3: ACADEMIC RECORDS

3.1 Student Numbering System
Each student is assigned a unique identification number upon enrollment:
- Format: YYYY-NNNN (Year of enrollment + sequential number)
- Example: 2022-0001 (first student enrolled in 2022)

3.2 Grade Records
- Grades are recorded in the student information system within five (5) working days after submission by faculty
- Faculty must submit grades through the faculty portal or in hard copy to the Registrar's Office
- Grades are final once recorded and signed by the Department Head
- Grade changes require approval from the Department Head and Academic Affairs

3.3 Transcription
- The Registrar's Office prepares official transcripts based on the student's academic record
- Transcripts are issued upon request with proper identification
- Processing time: three (3) to five (5) working days for regular requests
- Rush processing: one (1) to two (2) working days (with additional fee)

3.4 Academic Records Confidentiality
- Student academic records are confidential and protected under the Data Privacy Act
- Only the student, authorized school officials, and authorized government agencies may access student records
- Releasing student information to third parties requires written consent from the student

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 4: DOCUMENT ISSUANCE

4.1 Available Documents
The Registrar's Office issues the following documents:
- Transcript of Records
- Diploma (upon graduation)
- Certificate of Enrollment
- Certificate of Good Moral Character
- Certificate of Transfer/Crediting
- Certified True Copy of Grades
- Diploma Authentication
- Scholastic Record

4.2 Request Process
Step 1: Fill out the Document Request Form
Step 2: Submit the form to the Document Processing Section
Step 3: Pay the corresponding fee at the Accounting Office
Step 4: Present the official receipt to the Document Processing Section
Step 5: Claim the document on the scheduled release date

4.3 Processing Time
- Regular processing: five (5) working days
- Rush processing: one (1) to two (2) working days (with additional fee of 50% of regular fee)
- Diploma release: within thirty (30) working days after graduation

4.4 Fees
- Transcript of Records: PHP 50.00 per copy
- Certificate of Enrollment: PHP 30.00 per copy
- Certificate of Good Moral Character: PHP 30.00 per copy
- Certified True Copy of Grades: PHP 30.00 per copy
- Diploma: included in graduation fee
- Diploma Authentication: PHP 100.00 per copy
- Rush processing: additional 50% of regular fee

4.5 Requirements for Document Request
- Valid government-issued ID
- Authorization letter (if requesting on behalf of another person)
- Special power of attorney (if requesting for a third party)
- Official receipt of payment

SECTION 5: GRADUATION

5.1 Graduation Requirements
Students must meet the following requirements to graduate:
- Completion of all required courses in the program curriculum
- Minimum cumulative grade point average as required by the program
- No outstanding financial obligations with TMC
- Clearance from all offices (Library, Accounting, Student Affairs, etc.)
- Submission of all required documents (thesis, portfolio, OJT completion, etc.)
- Payment of graduation fees

5.2 Application for Graduation
- Students must file their application for graduation at the Registrar's Office
- Filing period: as announced by the Academic Calendar (usually during the second semester)
- Required documents:
  - Application for Graduation form
  - Certificate of completion of all academic requirements
  - Clearance form signed by all concerned offices
  - 2x2 ID pictures (for diploma)
  - OJT completion certificate (for programs with practicum)

5.3 Academic Honors
TMC recognizes academic honors for graduating students:
- Summa Cum Laude: weighted average of 1.00 to 1.25
- Magna Cum Laude: weighted average of 1.26 to 1.50
- Cum Laude: weighted average of 1.51 to 1.75
- With Honors: weighted average of 1.76 to 2.00

Requirements for honors:
- No grade lower than 2.00 in any course
- Completion of the program within the prescribed period
- No records of academic dishonesty or disciplinary action

5.4 Graduation Ceremonies
- Commencement exercises are held at the end of each academic year
- Graduates are required to attend the ceremony
- Academic attire rental is available through authorized suppliers
- A graduation fee is charged to cover ceremony expenses

5.5 Diploma Release
- Diplomas are released within thirty (30) working days after the graduation ceremony
- Diplomas may be claimed personally by the graduate or by an authorized representative
- Unclaimed diplomas after one (1) year will be held by the Registrar's Office

SECTION 6: SCHOLARSHIP AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

6.1 Types of Scholarships
TMC offers the following scholarship programs:
- CHED Unified Student Financial Assistance System for Tertiary Education (UniFAST)
- TMC Academic Scholarship (for high-performing students)
- TMC Athletic Scholarship (for varsity athletes)
- TMC Cultural Scholarship (for cultural performers)
- LGU Scholarship (for residents of Trinidad, Bohol)
- Private Scholarship (from external organizations)

6.2 Eligibility Requirements
- Must be a Filipino citizen
- Must be enrolled or planning to enroll at TMC
- Must meet the specific requirements of the chosen scholarship
- Must not be receiving another scholarship covering the same expenses

6.3 Application Process
Step 1: Secure a scholarship application form from the Registrar's Office
Step 2: Complete the form and attach all required documents
Step 3: Submit the application to the Scholarship Committee
Step 4: Undergo evaluation and interview
Step 5: Receive notification of approval or denial

6.4 Scholarship Benefits
- Full or partial tuition fee waiver
- Monthly stipend (for selected scholarships)
- Book allowance
- Uniform allowance
- Transportation allowance (for selected scholarships)

6.5 Scholarship Maintenance
- Maintain a general weighted average of at least 85%
- No grade lower than 80% in any subject
- No records of disciplinary action
- Active participation in scholarship-related activities
- Submission of periodic progress reports

SECTION 7: ENROLLMENT STATISTICS AND REPORTING

7.1 Enrollment Reports
The Registrar's Office prepares the following reports:
- Daily enrollment summary
- Weekly enrollment progress report
- Final enrollment statistics per semester
- Annual enrollment trend analysis
- CHED enrollment report (ICTRHEI)

7.2 Data Submission
- Enrollment data is submitted to CHED through the Information System for Consumer Regulation in Higher Education (ISCRETHEI)
- Data must be accurate and submitted on or before the deadline
- Any discrepancy must be reported and corrected immediately

7.3 Student Information System
- TMC uses an integrated student information system for enrollment, grading, and records management
- All faculty and staff must be trained on the system
- Data backup is performed daily
- System maintenance is scheduled during non-peak hours

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 8: DATA PRIVACY AND RECORDS MANAGEMENT

8.1 Data Privacy
- Student records are protected under Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- Only authorized personnel may access student records
- Student information shall not be released to third parties without written consent
- Data breaches must be reported immediately to the Data Protection Officer

8.2 Records Retention
- Student academic records are retained permanently
- Enrollment records are retained for ten (10) years
- Financial records are retained for seven (7) years
- Transitory records are retained for three (3) years

8.3 Digital Records
- Digital copies of academic records are stored in the student information system
- Physical copies are stored in fireproof cabinets
- Regular backup is performed on external drives and cloud storage
- Access to digital records is logged and audited
